FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No! Kharcha is designed specifically for manual entry so you maintain absolute privacy and mindfulness over every single rupee spent.

How long does it take to log an expense?

Less than 5 seconds. Just type what you spent in plain text (e.g., "lunch 350"), and our parser instantly records and categorizes it.

Is my financial data secure?

Since we don't connect to financial institutions or store bank logins, your exposure is zero. Your data is encrypted and private.

Can I customize my monthly budget categories?

Yes, you can easily set custom limits for dining out, transport, shopping, utilities, and any custom category you prefer.
